Name: Ryoga Chinen
Role: The protagonist and the first nine star hunter to reach the top without a constellation who regresses to the past.
Appearance: Tall with messy black hair and sharp calculating eyes wearing a dark button up shirt and later a black casual t shirt.
Personality: Determined defiant and incredibly cold when dealing with his enemies who seek to control him.
--------------------
Name: Dmitry Andreevich Romanov
Role: A nine star hunter and the incarnation of Odin who serves as a primary antagonist from the seven great families.
Appearance: An elderly man with a long grey beard and hair wearing a large brown hooded robe with glowing blue eyes.
Personality: Arrogant and elitist believing that only those with the grace of a constellation have the right to power.
