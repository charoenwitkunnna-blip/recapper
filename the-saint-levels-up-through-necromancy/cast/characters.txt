Name: Ryoga Chinen
Role: The protagonist and a 9-star Hunter known as the Undead King who regresses to the past.
Appearance: A young man with sharp features, short dark hair, and intense golden eyes. He wears a black shirt with brown leather straps and later a black jacket over a dark t-shirt.
Personality: Cold, strategic, and incredibly resilient with a heart that remains unshaken even in the face of gods.

Name: Dmitry Andreevich Romanov
Role: A 9-star Hunter and the incarnation of Odin who leads the Romanov family.
Appearance: An elderly man with long flowing white hair and a thick beard. He wears a distinctive red hooded cloak over white robes and his eyes glow with blue celestial light.
Personality: Arrogant, elitist, and completely devoted to the power of the constellations.
