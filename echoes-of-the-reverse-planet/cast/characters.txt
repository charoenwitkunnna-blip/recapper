Name: Baek Woohyun
Role: Protagonist and survivor of Project: Transcendence.
Appearance: Initially thin with short dark hair, later becomes extremely muscular with long, messy black hair and a cold gaze.
Personality: Resilient, trauma-hardened, and single-mindedly focused on revenge for his fallen friends.
--------------------
Name: Soo-ah
Role: Woohyun's friend whose death fuels his drive for vengeance.
Appearance: A young girl with long pink hair, often seen in a white experimental gown.
Personality: Kind-hearted and self-sacrificing, even in her final moments.
--------------------
Name: The Red-Haired Guard
Role: The primary antagonist during the initial escape attempt.
Appearance: A massive, muscular man with spiked red hair, wearing a high-tech guard suit and tactical goggles.
Personality: Cruel, arrogant, and sadistic, enjoying the slaughter of weak test subjects.
--------------------
Name: Masked Scientists
Role: The administrative enforcers of Project: Transcendence.
Appearance: Wearing white hazmat suits and black gas masks, often carrying electronic tablets.
Personality: Cold, detached, and viewing human subjects as mere data points.
