Name: Baek Woohyun
Role: The protagonist and sole successful survivor of Project: Transcendence.
Appearance: Initially a typical gamer, but evolves into a tall, extremely muscular man with long, messy black hair and cold, glowing eyes.
Personality: Driven by trauma and a singular focus on revenge, yet deeply haunted by the guilt of his friends' deaths.
--------------------
Name: Soo-Ah
Role: Woohyun's close friend and the emotional catalyst for his vengeance.
Appearance: A young woman with long pink hair, seen in tattered white prisoner clothing and strapped to a torture chair.
Personality: Selfless and kind, choosing to apologize and encourage Woohyun even in her final moments.
--------------------
Name: The Red-Haired Guard
Role: A high-level enforcer for the lab who executes the escapees.
Appearance: A massive, muscular man with spiky red hair and a beard, wearing green-lensed tactical goggles and a black 'Guard' bodysuit.
Personality: Arrogant, sadistic, and takes immense pleasure in slaughtering those he deems weaker.
--------------------
Name: The Gas-Masked Scientists
Role: The primary antagonists overseeing the inhumane biological experiments of Project: Transcendence.
Appearance: Figures wearing white lab coats and black industrial gas masks, often seen holding tablets or monitoring equipment.
Personality: Cold, clinical, and completely devoid of empathy for their human test subjects.
--------------------
Name: The Escapee Friends
Role: Fellow victims of the lab who sacrifice their lives to allow Woohyun to escape.
Appearance: Various men and women in bloodstained white prisoner jumpsuits, carrying makeshift weapons like pipes and axes.
Personality: Desperate but loyal, driven by a mutual bond of suffering and a final wish for revenge.
--------------------
Name: Kim Youngjun
Role: Experiment No. 81 and fellow survivor who seeks immediate and brutal retribution against their captors.
Appearance: A tall man with spiky light-colored hair, wearing a black tactical bodysuit and combat gear.
Personality: Aggressive, impulsive, and deeply scarred by the experiments, serving as a foil to Woohyun's more calculated nature.
--------------------
Name: Jung Nayeon
Role: Woohyun's closest friend in the real world and an aspiring gaming streamer.
Appearance: A young woman with long brown hair, often wearing casual clothes like a pink baseball cap and oversized t-shirts.
Personality: Cheerful, energetic, and slightly bossy, she acts as Woohyun's primary link to his former normal life.
--------------------
Name: General Manager
Role: A high-ranking official overseeing Project: Transcendence.
Appearance: A middle-aged man with short blond hair and a stubble beard, wearing a formal blue suit and tie.
Personality: Arrogant and elitist when in power, but turns cowardly and desperate when faced with the consequences of his actions.
--------------------
Name: Kang Soyul
Role: A professional gaming streamer and Woohyun's accidental party member.
Appearance: A young woman with long, dark blue hair and bright eyes. She wears a white research lab prisoner outfit similar to Woohyun's, but looks much more like a modern gamer.
Personality: Energetic, dramatic, and highly motivated by money and her viewers' opinions, though she is currently terrified by the game's difficulty.
--------------------
Name: The Bureau Soldier
Role: An ally within the game tutorial who provides Woohyun with equipment.
Appearance: A soldier in dark tactical armor with a 'Dimensional Management Bureau' logo. He is seen heavily injured and bleeding out against a wall.
Personality: Selfless and dutiful, prioritizing the safety of the 'victims' even as he faces death.
