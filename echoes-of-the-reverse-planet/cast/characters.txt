Name: Baek Woohyun
Role: Main Protagonist
Appearance: Initially average build with black hair; later becomes highly muscular with long, messy black hair and an intense, haunted gaze.
Personality: Driven by vengeance and extreme survivor's guilt, he is a resilient warrior who has lost his humanity to the horrors of the game.
--------------------
Name: Soo-ah
Role: Woohyun's deceased friend
Appearance: Young woman with long pink hair, often seen in distress or tied to the electric chair during Woohyun's traumatic flashbacks.
Personality: Selfless and protective of Woohyun, she remains his primary motivation for revenge.
--------------------
Name: Red-haired Guard
Role: Minor Antagonist
Appearance: Muscular man with spiky red hair, a matching beard, and high-tech green goggles.
Personality: Sadistic and arrogant, he takes pleasure in slaughtering the escaping test subjects.
