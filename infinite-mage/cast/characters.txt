Name: Arian Shirone
Role: Protagonist
Appearance: A 12-year-old boy with messy blonde hair and striking, bright blue eyes. He wears simple commoner's clothes.
Personality: Extremely curious, intelligent, and determined. Despite his humble upbringing, he is self-taught and possesses a deep thirst for knowledge about magic.
--------------------
Name: Arian Vincent
Role: Shirone's Father
Appearance: A 40-year-old muscular man with long dark hair, a beard, and a rugged hunter's build. Often seen carrying an axe or heavy loads of wood.
Personality: A protective and hardworking man. He is humble and feels guilty for his lack of status, but loves Shirone unconditionally as his own son.
--------------------
Name: Olina
Role: Shirone's Mother
Appearance: A woman with long, warm reddish-brown hair and a kind face.
Personality: Gentle and nurturing, she shares Vincent's love for their adopted son.
--------------------
Name: Myrhe Alpheas
Role: Headmaster of Alpheas Magic Academy
Appearance: An elderly man with a long white beard and hair, wearing blue and gold mage robes. He is a Grade 4 Certified Mage.
Personality: Wise, observant, and welcoming. He values 'Insight' over mere status or hard work.
--------------------
Name: Shuamin
Role: Student at Alpheas Magic Academy
Appearance: A young girl with long, purple hair tied into two neat braids. She wears the blue and black uniform of the Alpheas Magic Academy.
Personality: Diligent and respectful. She is a talented student capable of entering the 'Spirit Zone' to showcase magical basics.
