Name: Vincent
Role: Shirone's adoptive father and a hunter.
Appearance: A tall, muscular man with long dark hair and a rugged beard, often carrying a hunting axe.
Personality: Protective, humble, and hardworking; he loves his adopted son unconditionally.

Name: Arian Shirone
Role: The protagonist who discovers a talent for magic.
Appearance: A 12-year-old boy with messy blonde hair and large, expressive blue eyes.
Personality: Intelligent, curious, and sensitive; he possesses a deep thirst for knowledge despite his low social status.
--------------------
Name: Myrhe Alpheas
Role: Headmaster of the Alpheas Magic Academy.
Appearance: An elderly man with a long white beard and hair, wearing ornate blue and black robes.
Personality: Wise, observant, and welcoming; he is a high-level certified mage.
--------------------
Name: Shuamin
Role: A student at Alpheas Magic Academy who demonstrates basic magic sensitivity.
Appearance: A young girl student with long purple hair tied in two braids, wearing the Alpheas Academy uniform.
Personality: Diligent and obedient, she is a talented student capable of entering the Spirit Zone.
