Name: Arian Vincent
Role: Shirone's adoptive father. A hardworking hunter/woodcutter living deep in the mountains.
Appearance: A tall, extremely muscular man with long dark hair, a goatee, and tanned skin. Often seen wearing simple hunter's clothing and carrying a heavy axe.
Personality: Protective, humble, and fiercely loving toward his son, despite his rugged and intimidating exterior.
--------------------
Name: Olina
Role: Vincent's wife and Shirone's adoptive mother.
Appearance: A kind-looking woman with long, reddish-brown hair, typically dressed in humble village attire.
Personality: Nurturing and warm, she provides the emotional core of the Arian family.
--------------------
Name: Arian Shirone
Role: The protagonist. An abandoned child found in the mountains who possesses an insatiable curiosity for magic.
Appearance: A young boy of 12 with messy blonde hair and large, expressive blue eyes. He is lean and looks more like a scholar than a hunter's son.
Personality: Intelligent, diligent, and brave. He is undeterred by social status and driven by a pure desire for knowledge.
--------------------
Name: Myrhe Alpheas
Role: Headmaster of the Alpheas Magic Academy and a Grade 4 Certified Mage.
Appearance: An elderly man with a long white beard and hair, wearing ornate blue and gold mage robes. He exudes wisdom and authority.
Personality: Eccentric, observant, and seemingly welcoming to those with genuine potential, regardless of their background.
