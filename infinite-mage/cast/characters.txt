Name: Arian Vincent
Role: Shirone's adoptive father and a skilled hunter living in the mountains.
Appearance: A tall, incredibly muscular man with long dark hair, a beard, and wearing common hunter's attire. He often carries a large wood-chopping axe.
Personality: Gruff and intimidating on the outside but deeply caring and protective of his family. He feels insecure about his lack of education compared to his son's brilliance.
--------------------
Name: Olina
Role: Vincent's wife and Shirone's adoptive mother.
Appearance: A woman with long reddish-brown hair and a kind face, typically wearing simple village clothing.
Personality: Gentle, nurturing, and supportive of her husband and child.
--------------------
Name: Arian Shirone
Role: The protagonist, a child found abandoned who possesses an insatiable curiosity for magic and high intelligence.
Appearance: A 12-year-old boy with messy blonde hair and striking blue eyes. He wears simple brown commoner clothes and is often seen clutching a large red book.
Personality: Inquisitive, polite, and determined. Despite the discrimination he faces as a commoner, he remains focused on his dream of understanding magic.
--------------------
Name: Myrhe Alpheas
Role: Headmaster of the Alpheas Magic Academy and a Grade 4 Certified Mage.
Appearance: An elderly man with long white hair and a flowing white beard, wearing elegant blue and gold academic robes.
Personality: Wise, observant, and seemingly welcoming, showing interest in those who possess a unique perspective on magic regardless of their status.
