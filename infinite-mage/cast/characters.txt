Name: Arian Shirone
Role: The protagonist, a foundling with a genius-level intellect and a mysterious talent for magic.
Appearance: A 12-year-old boy with messy blonde hair and bright blue eyes, wearing simple brown commoner's clothing.
Personality: Curious, polite, and deeply determined to learn despite the social barriers he faces.
--------------------
Name: Arian Vincent
Role: Shirone's adoptive father who works as a woodcutter and hunter.
Appearance: A 40-year-old muscular hunter with long dark hair, a beard, and numerous scars across his powerful build.
Personality: Dignified and fiercely protective of his son, hiding a soft heart behind a rugged, imposing exterior.
--------------------
Name: Myrhe Alpheas
Role: The Headmaster of Alpheas Magic Academy and a Grade 4 Certified Mage.
Appearance: An elderly man with a long white beard and hair, dressed in ornate blue and gold mage robes.
Personality: Wise, perceptive, and egalitarian, valuing innate insight over social status or wealth.
--------------------
Name: Olina
Role: Shirone's adoptive mother and Vincent's wife.
Appearance: A kind-faced woman with reddish-brown hair who wears a simple, warm cloak.
Personality: Nurturing, supportive, and deeply loving toward her adoptive son.
