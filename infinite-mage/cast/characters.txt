Name: Arian Vincent
Role: Shirone's adoptive father and a skilled mountain hunter.
Appearance: Extremely muscular build, long dark hair, facial hair, often wears a hunter's vest and carries an axe.
Personality: Protective, hardworking, and deeply loving despite his rough exterior.
--------------------
Name: Olina
Role: Vincent's wife and Shirone's adoptive mother.
Appearance: Kind-faced woman with long orange hair, typically wearing simple village clothes.
Personality: Nurturing and supportive.
--------------------
Name: Arian Shirone
Role: The protagonist, an abandoned child raised by hunters who dreams of magic.
Appearance: Young boy with messy blonde hair and striking, bright blue eyes. Often seen clutching a book.
Personality: Incredibly intelligent, polite, resilient, and obsessed with learning.
--------------------
Name: Myrhe Alpheas
Role: Headmaster of the Alpheas Magic Academy and a Grade 4 Certified Mage.
Appearance: Elderly man with a very long white beard and hair, wearing blue and gold scholarly robes.
Personality: Wise, observant, and seemingly welcoming to those with curiosity.
