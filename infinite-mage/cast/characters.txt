Name: Arian Vincent
Role: Shirone's adoptive father and a hardworking mountain hunter.
Appearance: A massive, 40-year-old man with a muscular build, long dark hair tied back, and a rugged beard. He typically wears hunter's garb and carries a large axe.
Personality: Protective, humble, and doting. He deeply loves his family and tries his best to provide for his son's intellectual needs despite his own lack of education.
--------------------
Name: Arian Shirone
Role: The protagonist, a genius-level commoner boy with an insatiable thirst for magic.
Appearance: A 12-year-old boy with messy blond hair and bright blue eyes. He wears simple, brown commoner clothes and is often seen clutching a book.
Personality: Extremely intelligent, curious, and resilient. Despite being mocked by others, he remains focused on his dream of becoming a mage.
--------------------
Name: Olina
Role: Vincent's wife and Shirone's adoptive mother.
Appearance: A woman with reddish-brown hair, often seen wearing a simple cloak or house dress.
Personality: Kind, nurturing, and grateful for the family she finally received.
--------------------
Name: Myrhe Alpheas
Role: Headmaster of the Alpheas Magic Academy and a Grade 4 Certified Mage.
Appearance: An elderly man with a very long white beard and hair, wearing ornate blue and black mage robes. He carries himself with great dignity.
Personality: Wise, observant, and welcoming of those who possess true 'Insight,' regardless of their social standing.
