Christian pushes his body to the absolute brink as a terrifying dark aura explodes from his training blade. He unleashes a devastating blow that sends shockwaves through the entire arena as he surpasses all physical limitations. The crowd gasps as a massive tornado of flame and dark energy consumes the training grounds in a violent display of power. Maisak looks on in complete disbelief as her unstoppable explosion technique is completely neutralized by a power she cannot understand. In a flash of dark energy Christian dominates the battlefield proving he is no longer the weakling they once mocked. The high ranking nobles are terrified to realize that Christians black sword is capable of cultivating five star destruction demonic energy. Even the Grand Patriarch cannot hide his amazement wondering if he is witnessing the legendary dark steel energy from a mere child. Maisak is left reeling and bloodied unable to comprehend how a notorious scumbag has suddenly reached the five star realm. She collapses into the dirt coughing up foam as her arrogance is shattered into a thousand pieces by Christians overwhelming resolve. The Grand Patriarch smiles noting that his trip to see this competition was well worth the effort to discover such a hidden powerhouse. Christian immediately reverts to his mask of a cheerful and loyal successor to keep his true strength and knowledge of the future hidden. As he walks away Christian reflects on his success in finally manifesting dark energy even if it was only a temporary measure. This power grants him the ability to suppress any target by blasting them with demonic energy regardless of the distance. Suddenly a chilling presence washes over him and Christians body refuses to move as every nerve in his body begins to scream in terror. Out of the shadows emerges Serad the eldest brother and the most dangerous lunatic of the entire Dark Mage clan. Rania remembers him as a complete madman who went missing long before the great war broke out. Serad is the top disciple of Simon and the ruthless commander of the Blood Shadow Squad who carries an aura of pure death. Serad stares down at Christian questioning if this is truly their first time meeting in this life with a mocking smile. In the previous life it was this man who stood over the broken body of Evans companion laughing as he carved them limb from limb. Serad claims that if Christian had actually mastered swordsmanship or magic he would have been much more entertaining than Evan was. Christian is consumed by a cold rage realizing he must kill this monster no matter what to prevent the future tragedy. Serad grabs Christian by the neck mocking his aggressive stance and warning him not to bare his claws too much against family. He claims that Christian is far below his expectations and that his recent growth is nothing special compared to true monsters. Christian smiles back with a murderous gaze promising that he will personally take Serads head very soon. Serad laughs at the challenge but warns Christian that he is not yet enough to hold his interest for long. With a simple flick of his finger Serad draws blood from Christians forehead as a final warning of his overwhelming superiority. Serad walks away leaving Christian in the dust while stating he is not exactly known for having much patience. Christian slams his fist into the stone wall consumed by the rage and helplessness of staring right at the one who slaughtered his comrades. He realizes that Serad has not yet made his contract with the demon king which means he still has a chance to win. Christian resolves that he has exactly one year to grow strong enough to ensure that Serad dies once and for all.
