Name: Zhang Yuanqing
Role: The protagonist who suffers from a unique brain condition that hyper-processes information.
Appearance: An athletic young man with messy black hair and sharp blue eyes.
Personality: Analytical, observant, and deeply loyal to his childhood friends.
--------------------
Name: Lei Yibing
Role: Yuanqing's childhood protector and 'brother' figure who has mysteriously vanished.
Appearance: A young man with spiky orange-red hair and a fierce expression.
Personality: Brave, protective, and hot-headed when it comes to defending his friends.
--------------------
Name: Yuanqing's Mother
Role: The protagonist's mother who keeps the household running with a firm hand.
Appearance: A middle-aged woman with short white hair, often seen wearing a pink apron.
Personality: Strict, traditional, and constantly worried about her family's future.
--------------------
Name: Aunt Jiang
Role: Yuanqing's aunt who lives with the family.
Appearance: A beautiful young woman with long, flowing light blue hair and a playful demeanor.
Personality: Teasing, mischievous, and experts at avoiding her mother's marriage setups.
--------------------
Name: Wang Tai
Role: A Captain from the Kangyang District Public Security Bureau investigating Lei Yibing's disappearance.
Appearance: An older man with grey hair, a thin goatee, wearing a fur-lined red pinstripe suit and holding a wine glass.
Personality: Shrewd, intimidating, and observant, with a flair for the dramatic.
--------------------
Name: Guan Ya
Role: An investigator and partner to Wang Tai who specializes in digital forensics.
Appearance: A tall woman with long, sleek reddish-orange hair, wearing a white button-down shirt and a black pencil skirt with stockings.
Personality: Sharp-witted, teasing, and highly efficient at uncovering secrets.
--------------------
Name: Undead Miner
Role: The reanimated remains of the workers who disappeared in the Yuling Tunnel urban legend.
Appearance: A desiccated corpse with grey, rotting skin, wearing tattered worker's clothing and a mining helmet with a dead light.
Personality: Mindless and eerie, they seem to follow a mysterious directive rather than attacking immediately.
--------------------
Name: Temple Deity Statue
Role: The central figure of the mysterious temple Zhang Yuanqing must explore.
Appearance: A massive, imposing stone statue of a long-haired figure in flowing robes, holding a staff, flanked by smaller attendant statues with eerie, blank eyes.
Personality: Immovable and ominous, radiating a sense of ancient, silent judgment.
