Name: Mu-myeong
Role: The protagonist and loyal escort warrior who regresses into his masters body.
Appearance: Long dark hair, initially in rags as a child, then a handsome warrior with a lean build and cold eyes.
Personality: Extremely disciplined, stoic, and fiercely loyal until his betrayal.
--------------------
Name: Cheon Wicang
Role: The former Patriarch of the Cheon Clan who gave Mu-myeong his purpose.
Appearance: An elderly man with a long beard wearing regal brown and red patriarch robes.
Personality: Authoritative, traditional, and far-sighted.
--------------------
Name: Cheon Muryang
Role: The dissolute eldest young master of the Cheon Clan and the original owner of the body.
Appearance: Initially a chubby child in red robes, later an obese man with a messy beard and greedy expression.
Personality: Cowardly, lazy, and treacherous.
--------------------
Name: Wol-yeong
Role: The personal maid of the eldest young master.
Appearance: A young woman with long dark hair tied back, wearing a simple yellow and red maid outfit.
Personality: Timid, fearful, and long-suffering due to years of abuse.
--------------------
Name: Neung Ryowoon
Role: An arrogant disciple and bully who frequently mocks Muryang and bets on his failures.
Appearance: A young man with long brown hair tied back and a permanent smug expression, wearing the blue robes of a Cheon Clan disciple.
Personality: Cruel, opportunistic, and disrespectful toward those he considers beneath him.
--------------------
Name: Cheon Seon-hak
Role: General Commander of the Heavenly Dragon and Heavenly Phoenix Sword Units and Muryangs uncle.
Appearance: A middle-aged man with a thick grey beard and hair, wearing red and black commander robes with a large dark cape.
Personality: Stern, suspicious, and highly critical of Muryang, believing him to be beyond redemption.
--------------------
Name: Lee Seok-gi
Role: The head disciple of the Cheon Clan and a secret traitor.
Appearance: A man with a sharp face and a black headband who wears blue disciple robes.
Personality: Smug, arrogant, and deceptive.
--------------------
Name: Cha Myeong-hak
Role: Mu-myeongs first official duel opponent who holds a deep grudge from a past encounter.
Appearance: A young man with long dark hair tied up and a prominent diagonal scar across his left eye and cheek wearing blue disciple robes.
Personality: Resentful, vengeful, and determined to prove his strength against the reformed young master.
--------------------
Name: Hwa hong
Role: A servant of the Cheon Clan and a close friend of Wol yeong.
Appearance: A young woman with her hair tied in two buns wearing a pink and white maid outfit.
Personality: Loyal, emotional, and prone to panic when her friends are in danger.
--------------------
Name: Tang So so
Role: The powerful and high status fiancee of Cheon Muryang.
Appearance: A beautiful woman with cold blue eyes wearing elaborate silver head ornaments and traditional tribal patterned robes.
Personality: Arrogant, cruel, and cold hearted towards those she considers inferior.
--------------------
Name: Tang Clan Warrior
Role: A powerful first-rate martial artist and protector of Tang So-hye.
Appearance: A tall man with a thick black beard and a blue and red headband wearing green and grey warrior robes.
Personality: Arrogant, lethal, and strictly follows the laws of his clan.
--------------------
Name: Cheon Clan Spy
Role: A snitch and informant working secretly for Neung Ryowoon.
Appearance: A young disciple with a simple top knot wearing the blue robes of the Cheon Clan.
Personality: Sneaky, cowardly, and opportunistic.
--------------------
Name: Traitor Trainees
Role: Henchmen sent to poison Mu-myeong and ruin his reputation before the banquet.
Appearance: A group of men wearing blue Cheon Clan disciple robes with one having a rough beard and a messy topknot.
Personality: Sneaky, arrogant, and overconfident in their deceptive plan.
--------------------
Name: Cheon Clan Head Trainee
Role: An opportunistic disciple and minor antagonist.
Appearance: A man with sharp features and a smug smile wearing a black cap and the blue robes of the Cheon Clan.
Personality: Greedy, ambitious, and willing to switch sides for personal gain.
--------------------
Name: Elder Tang
Role: A high ranking representative of the Tang Clan.
Appearance: An older man with a thick greyish-purple beard and hair wearing a yellow turban and green and blue robes.
Personality: Hot-tempered, proud, and quick to use force to defend his clan's honor.
--------------------
Name: Duel Referee
Role: The official responsible for starting and monitoring the martial arts duel.
Appearance: A middle-aged man with a thin mustache wearing grey and black robes with a simple top knot.
Personality: Strict, loud, and impartial.
