The sound of clashing steel echoes across the plains as the human vanguard desperately holds the line against the orcish horde. Just when hope seems to fade a familiar banner crests the horizon. Prince Yuri and the Briol knights have arrived to turn the tide. Yuri wastes no time shouting for his men to pick up the pace and follow him straight into the mouth of the beast. Spotting a massive siege tower the prince orders his troops to use it immediately to breach the enemy fortress. With a burst of incredible speed Yuri lunges forward with his spear whistling through the air as it finds its mark in an orcs chest. The prince leaps onto the high ramparts looking down upon a sea of monsters that are clearly being manipulated by a forbidden power. He realizes the enemy has hidden a powerful sorcerer within the walls who is using black magic to turn these grunts into tireless killing machines. Yuri signals his subordinates to raise the royal flag informing the supreme commander that the location of the orc king has finally been pinned down. Back at the alliance camp Prince Ragnar sees the signal and smirks with pride knowing his brother has done the impossible once again. Princess Sibila and her elite knights do not hesitate for a second as they draw their blades to join the slaughter and secure their place in history. Yuri gathers his core team for one final briefing and warns them that while the main army holds the line they must take Orcuas head. He gives his men a simple command to not die before drawing his legendary blade and charging into the final pocket of resistance. The Holy Kingdom knights move with surgical precision carving through the orc ranks like a hot knife through butter as the assault peaks. Suddenly a vortex of emerald flames erupts on the battlefield as Master Murong Chan enters the fray and incinerates dozens of monsters with a single swing. Yuri carves a path of destruction with his blue mana but he cannot shake a growing sense of dread that something is terribly wrong. His fears are confirmed when a massive shadow looms over the battlefield radiating an aura of pure malice that freezes the blood of every soldier present. Orcua the Orc King finally reveals his true form and he has been mutated by dark magic into a literal demon of destruction. The monster laughs and claims that Yuri has fallen right into his trap and will soon become the ultimate sacrifice for his dark masters.
