Name: Lee Hyun Seok
Role: The protagonist and legendary hero who spent twenty years in another world before returning to Earth.
Appearance: A battle hardened man with a messy beard and scarred face wearing heavy silver armor and a tattered red cape. Later he inhabits a youthful body with short dark hair and sharp gold eyes.
Personality: Cynical and world weary but highly determined with a deep hatred for the corruption and cruelty of the other world.
--------------------
Name: The Saintess
Role: A supposed ally of the hero who is actually a traitorous manipulator.
Appearance: A beautiful young woman with long blonde hair and light brown eyes wearing a white and gold religious habit.
Personality: Deceptive and hateful toward outsiders believing herself superior to the hero she was supposed to support.
--------------------
Name: The Knight
Role: One of the hero companions who was bribed by the Emperor to kill him.
Appearance: A stern man with short dark hair and a small beard wearing functional plate armor.
Personality: Greedy and ambitious placing national treasures and status above loyalty.
--------------------
Name: The Sage
Role: A powerful mage and companion who turned against the hero.
Appearance: An elderly man with a long white beard and deep wrinkles wearing brown scholarly robes and carrying a staff.
Personality: Cruel and narrow minded with a passion for human experimentation and slavery.
--------------------
Name: The Goddess
Role: The deity who summoned the hero and controls the path between worlds.
Appearance: An ethereal being with long flowing golden hair and soft features surrounded by a divine yellow glow.
Personality: Enigmatic and manipulative viewing the hero and the world as pieces in a game.
--------------------
