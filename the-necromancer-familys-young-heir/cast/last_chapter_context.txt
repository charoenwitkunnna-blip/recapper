Leon glares at the knight with a piercing gaze and tells him the cold truth. You should have died a long time ago. Leon reveals that the distinct scent of a demon is reeking from him. It is a dark aura that should not exist on a living human. Using the forbidden black magic spell Mind Pierce Leon dives deep into the knights subconscious. It is a technique that strips away all secrets. It is a dangerous spell that reveals the raw emotions and deepest thoughts of those whose spirits are weak enough to be pierced. Leon senses a whirlwind of emotions within the knight. Anxiety unease and a crushing sense of resignation dominate his mind. Leon extends his hand and prepares to end the charade. He declares that he will have to put him down right here. But the knight does not beg for his own life. He begs for the safety of the young lady he serves. Jed draws his blade with a desperate resolve. He warns Leon that if he stands in his way he will have no choice but to resist. Leon scoffs and summons his undead army. Massive skeletons with glowing red eyes emerge from the shadows to surround the knight. Jed lunges forward with a strike born of sheer willpower but Leon easily blocks the blow with his dark energy. Leon sighs as he looks at the knight. He was hoping for a simple villain but this situation is far more complicated. Back in the tent Lady Flora notices the long silence and asks if everything is alright between the two men. She shares how Jed has been her protector since she was a small child and her only loyal ally after her father died. Even when everyone else abandoned her Jed was the only one who stayed by her side through the rain. He even lost his arm while shielding her from a vicious attack. His devotion is beyond any normal human measure. The guild master asks Flora if there is another reason she posted such a high reward for this mission. Back in the silence barrier Jed continues his desperate struggle against the overwhelming power of the necromancer. Leon easily dodges every strike. He notes that the knights basics are solid but he is not a great knight by any standard. Leon asks why he goes to such lengths to defy death. Most humans would simply accept the end out of fear. Jed reveals that he made a solemn promise to Floras late father to protect her with his life. Leon confirms the truth through his mind magic. The knights desire to protect Flora is genuine and absolute. Seeing such raw determination Leon decides to postpone Jeds death for a while. He is officially intrigued by this man. Leon realizes Jed is a rare case of a human using demonic power without fully turning into a monster like Tarell. A dark smile spreads across Leons face as he challenges the knight. He will decide Jeds fate based on the story he tells next.
